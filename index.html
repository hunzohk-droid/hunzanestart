How it works

From checkout to your own copy in a few minutes

Checkout is on Etsy. Your download is ready as soon as payment is confirmed. Nothing is posted to you.

The PDF guide has your template link. Open it with a free Google, Canva or Notion account (or in Word) and the copy is yours to edit.

Fill in the pale cells. Totals, charts and printable pages update by themselves. Printables come in A4 and US Letter.
